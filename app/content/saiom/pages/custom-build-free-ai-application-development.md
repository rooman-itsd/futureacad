## Why Upfront Agency Fees Kill Innovation

Most agencies want a large cheque before they write a line of code. You carry all the risk. If the tool never gets used, or never pays back, the money is gone.

That is why good ideas stall. A business owner with a clear operational problem can't always put that much cash on the line before seeing anything work.

Custom Build turns that around. For a standard build, **FutureAcad develops the application at no cost to you**. We do it for solutions we can also offer to other organisations. You pay only for hosting and usage once the application is live.

So you pay when the application is running and delivering value, not before.

## From Business Problem to Live Production in 6 to 8 Weeks

1. **Submit your problem**: Describe the operational bottleneck on the [Submit a Problem](/build/submit-a-problem) page. Plain language is fine. You don't need a technical spec.
2. **Get a reply and a free scoping call**: We reply within 5 business days. A free scoping call follows, where we agree what to build and whether it fits a standard or an exclusive build.
3. **Agile build sprint**: Our engineers build in short sprints. You get a working first version in 6 to 8 weeks and can shape it as it grows.
4. **Go live**: Your application goes into production. From go-live, you pay for hosting and usage. Support terms are agreed with you during scoping.

## Transparent Pay-for-Use Hosting and Source Code Buyout

The model is simple.

- **Standard build:** free to build. The application is built on a reusable architecture that FutureAcad can also offer to others.
- **Exclusive build:** if you want the solution to be yours alone, a basic development fee applies.
- **After go-live:** you pay monthly for hosting and usage. The amount depends on what we build and how much it is used.

You also keep an open option to buy out the source code and IP rights. The price depends on what was built, and we'll put the figures in writing on the scoping call so there are no surprises later.

For the current pricing structure, see the [Custom Build pricing page](/build/custom-ai-builds/pricing).

## Frequently Asked Questions About Custom Builds

### Is a Custom Build really free?

For a standard build, yes. There is no upfront development fee. We build it because the solution is reusable and FutureAcad can offer it to others. You pay for hosting and usage after go-live. An exclusive build carries a basic development fee.

### Who owns the intellectual property and source code?

On a standard build, the solution is built on a reusable architecture that FutureAcad can offer to others. If you want full ownership, you can ask to buy out the source code and IP rights. We'll agree the terms in writing during scoping.

### What will hosting cost?

You pay monthly for hosting and usage once your application is live. The amount depends on what we build and how much it is used. We'll give you a clear figure on the free scoping call, before you commit.

### How is my data kept private?

Our delivery follows ISO 27001. How your data is stored, who can reach it and where it is hosted are agreed with you during scoping and documented.

### What support do I get after go-live?

Support terms and response targets are agreed during scoping and written down, so you know what to expect from day one. Ask about them on the scoping call.

### How fast will I hear back?

We reply to every submitted problem within 5 business days. A free scoping call follows, and a working first version is typically ready in 6 to 8 weeks.

### What kind of problem should I submit?

Any operational bottleneck you think software or AI could fix, such as manual processes, missed follow-ups, slow reporting or disconnected systems. Describe what's going wrong and we'll work out whether it fits a Custom Build.

### Can FutureAcad build something that isn't a good fit for the free model?

Yes. If your needs are very specific or exclusive, we'll tell you plainly and discuss an exclusive build. Fixed-scope [AI Project Builds](/build/ai-project-builds) are another route.
