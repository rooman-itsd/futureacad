## What is the AI Readiness Audit?

It is a 4-week audit that tells you how ready your company is for AI, where the gaps are and which three use cases to do first.

You finish with numbers and a plan, not a list of ideas. Each of the top three use cases is ranked by NPV. The roadmap that follows covers 12 months and is costed.

It is built for CIOs who are being asked "what is our AI plan?" and want an answer they can defend to the board.

## Four deliverables. All of them usable.

### Where you stand

- **AI maturity score**: A clear score for your organisation today, so you start from a measured baseline.
- **Capability gap report**: What stands between your current setup and the AI you want to run in production.

### Where to go

- **Top three use cases, ranked by NPV**: The three opportunities most worth your budget, ordered by their value to the business.
- **Costed 12-month roadmap**: A sequenced plan with costs attached, ready to take into budget conversations.
