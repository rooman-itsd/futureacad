## Build an AI team in-house, or outsource a full AI function?

**Build in-house when:**
- AI is your product or your edge, and you want to own the models and the know-how.
- Your data or compliance rules keep the work inside your walls.
- You can hire, retain and manage a scarce team over several years.

**Outsource the function when:**
- The work is a repeatable operation with a clear unit: a seat, a transaction, a month.
- You want a service level, not a hiring plan.
- You want to pay for results and not carry the idle capacity of a team.

## Bengaluru mid-sized company: in-house GCC or a staffing partner with managed pods?

**A full in-house GCC** gives you maximum control, but you carry the set-up, the hiring and the management. It suits a company with a long horizon and the leadership to run it.

**A staffing partner** fills seats. FutureAcad offers permanent placement, contract hiring, managed pods and employer-of-record staffing from a pre-vetted engineer pool, in India and 13 international markets. See [Staffing Services](/staffing-gcc).

**A managed pod** is the middle path. [GCC-as-a-Service](/gcc-services) gives you a dedicated pod of 3–6 India-based engineers on a monthly bill. It scales up as you grow and ends in a Build–Operate–Transfer exit, so the team can become yours.

As a rule of thumb: if you need a few named hires, use staffing. If you need a working team that delivers and answers to you, use a pod. If you are sure you want your own centre, use pods with BOT as the way in.

## Which route fits your situation?

### Outsource the AI function

- **The work is a repeatable operation**: Customer service, finance, document processing, sales or HR operations are good fits.
- **You want to pay for volume or results**: Per seat, per transaction, per month, or per measured outcome.

### Build in-house

- **AI is core to what you sell**: You need to own the models, the data and the know-how.
- **You can hire and keep a team**: You have the leaders and the time to manage it.

### Managed pod or staffing (Bengaluru)

- **You need engineers working soon**: Use staffing for individual hires. Use a pod for a team that delivers.
- **You may want your own centre later**: A pod with a Build–Operate–Transfer exit keeps that door open.

## Questions buyers ask

### Is it better to build an AI team in-house or outsource?

It depends on the work. Outsource when the work is a repeatable operation you can measure. Build in-house when AI is core to your product or your data must stay inside. Many firms do both: they outsource operations and keep a small in-house team for strategy.

### What does it mean to outsource a full AI function billed per transaction?

A provider runs the whole function, for example customer service, finance, document processing, sales or HR operations, and you pay per seat, transaction or month against agreed SLAs. FutureAcad offers this as [Managed AI Functions](/transform/managed-ai-functions).

### Can I pay only when the AI delivers results?

Yes. [Outcome-Based AI](/transform/outcome-based-ai) is offered on gain-share, shared-savings or per-outcome terms. You pay only when a measured business result appears.

### Should a Bengaluru mid-sized company build its own GCC?

Build your own GCC if you have a long horizon and the leaders to run it. If you want engineers working sooner with less set-up, start with a pod of 3–6 engineers through [GCC Services](/gcc-services) and use the Build–Operate–Transfer exit later.

### What is the difference between a staffing partner and a managed engineering pod?

Staffing fills seats with permanent, contract or employer-of-record hires. A managed pod is a dedicated team that delivers work and is billed monthly. FutureAcad provides both.
