## What is *agentic AI*?

Agentic AI is software that is given a goal and works out how to reach it. It plans the steps, uses tools and your data, takes actions, and checks the result. A person sets the goal and the limits. The agent does the legwork.

- Works towards a goal, not one prompt at a time
- Uses tools such as your CRM, calendar or documents
- Asks a person to approve the steps you choose

## How is agentic AI different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers. You ask a question, it replies, and that is the end of it.

An AI agent acts. Ask it to qualify the leads that came in overnight and it can read each enquiry, check your CRM, score the lead, update the record and flag the best ones to your sales team.

The difference is not the language model underneath. It is what the system is allowed to do and how it decides the next step.

## How does an AI agent work?

Most agents follow the same loop.

1. **Goal**: A person states the outcome, for example "book qualified leads into the sales calendar".
2. **Plan**: The agent breaks the goal into steps.
3. **Act**: It calls tools, such as search, a database, an email system or a voice call.
4. **Check**: It reviews the result against the goal and tries again or escalates if something is off.
5. **Hand over**: It reports back, and a person approves anything you have marked as sensitive.

## What should you check before you build one?

Agents act on real systems, so a few decisions matter before any code is written.

- **Scope**: What exactly may the agent do, and what is off limits?
- **Data access**: Give it the minimum it needs and log what it touches.
- **Human approval**: Decide which steps need a person to say yes.
- **Measurement**: Agree the number that tells you it works, such as hours saved or leads handled.
- **Ownership**: Name who is accountable once it is live.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is agentic AI in simple terms?

Agentic AI is software that is given a goal and works out the steps to reach it. It can use tools, read your data and act, instead of only answering one question at a time.

### What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers what you ask. An AI agent takes a goal, plans several steps, calls tools such as your CRM or calendar, and completes the task, checking in with a person where you want approval.

### Is agentic AI safe to use with customer data?

It can be, if access is limited to what the agent needs, actions are logged, and risky steps need human approval. Decide these controls before the build, not after.
