## Seven tools that work together

### Import

Bring in Excel, CSV and JSON files, or connect databases and business apps with scheduled auto-sync.

### Auto Clean

One click removes duplicates, fixes text numbers and dates, fills missing values and handles outliers. Every change can be undone.

### Explore

Automatic data analysis with statistics, correlations and written findings.

### Visualise

Choose from 67 chart types, then build drag-and-drop dashboards, reports and presentations.

### Predict

Sales forecasting, AutoML and customer segmentation, all with no code.

### Ask BRO

Chat with an AI analyst in plain English, for example: "what were the top 5 products last quarter?"

### Act and share

Set alerts on targets and anomalies, share your work, and use team workspaces with role-based access.

## What's included in each step

### Auto Clean

- **Duplicates removed**: Repeated rows are taken out in one click.
- **Text numbers and dates fixed**: Values stored as text are turned into proper numbers and dates.
- **Missing values and outliers**: Gaps are filled and outliers handled, and you can undo any change.

### Predict

- **Sales forecasting**: Look ahead from your own sales data.
- **AutoML**: Machine learning without writing code.
- **Customer segmentation**: Group customers to see who is who.

### Act and share

- **Alerts**: Get notified about targets and anomalies.
- **Team workspaces**: Share work with your team and control who sees what with role-based access.
