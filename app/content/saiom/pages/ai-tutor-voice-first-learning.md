## What changes when you study by talking

A typed question-and-answer chat and a voice-first tutor feel very different in daily use. Here is how AI Tutor is built around the second.

### You explain, not just read

*Voice-first*

Speaking an answer aloud shows you where your understanding is thin. The tutor listens, then asks a follow-up question instead of giving the answer.

### Questions that lead you to the answer

*Socratic*

Socratic dialogue means the tutor guides you step by step. You reach the solution yourself and can check your own work.

### Your language, spoken and heard

*50+ languages*

### Hands-free, on your phone

*Mobile*

AI Tutor is mobile-optimised and works hands-free, so you can revise while commuting or walking, without a keyboard.

### Short sessions that stick

*Microlearning*

Lessons run 3–5 minutes. Adaptive quizzes and spaced repetition bring topics back at the right intervals.

### Move on when you are ready

*Mastery*

The engine confirms mastery before you advance. Your path adapts to your pace and gaps.

## How to study effectively with a tutor you talk to

1. **Say your goal out loud**: Tell the tutor what you are preparing for and where you feel weak. It uses this conversation to build your path.
2. **Answer in your own words**: When the tutor asks a question, answer aloud before asking for help. Wrong or half-right answers show exactly what to fix.
3. **Keep sessions short and frequent**: Use the 3–5 minute lessons several times a day instead of one long sitting. Spaced repetition handles the revisiting.
4. **Test yourself before moving on**: Take the adaptive quiz, check where you slipped, and advance only when the tutor says you are ready.
