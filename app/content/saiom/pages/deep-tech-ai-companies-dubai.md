## What is a deep tech AI company?

A deep tech AI company builds the technology itself: models wired into your data, agents that act on your systems, voice and interview platforms, and the engineering that keeps them reliable. It is different from an agency that resells a chatbot tool or a consultancy that stops at a slide deck.

For a buyer, the test is simple. Can they show AI products that are live today, and will they stay accountable after go-live?

## Six checks before you hire an AI company in Dubai

1. **Live products**: Ask to see systems running in production, not demos.
2. **Fixed scope and timeline**: Agree what will be delivered and when, before work starts.
3. **A named lead**: One accountable person, not a rotating team.
4. **Data and security**: Ask where your data is stored, who can reach it and which standards the delivery follows.
5. **Support after launch**: Ask what happens in the first weeks after go-live, and who fixes problems.
6. **Clear pricing**: Know what you pay to build and what you pay to run.

## Startup or large IT firm?

A focused AI startup usually moves faster and keeps senior engineers close to your project. A large firm can suit very broad programmes across many departments. For a first production AI system with a clear goal, speed and accountability usually matter most.
