## FutureAcad at a glance

- **What we are:** a deep tech startup. We pair frontier AI with enterprise-grade engineering and a global talent network to build systems that run in production.
- **Established:** 2025, in Dubai, UAE.
- **Base:** Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. [About FutureAcad](/about)
- **Delivery hubs:** Bengaluru, Dubai and Singapore, serving 13 markets in 4 regions. Bengaluru is a delivery hub, not our headquarters. [International delivery](/transform/international)
- **Compliance:** ISO 9001:2015, ISO 27001 and SEI-CMMI Level 3, in every hub.
- **Track record:** 7 live platforms shipped, 3 continents served.
- **Talent:** an alumni and engineer pool of 1.3M+ engineers, with OEM training partnerships with Microsoft, AWS, Google, Cisco, Red Hat and NVIDIA, and 10 global certifications.

**We sell AI to companies.** Our three disciplines are AI applications (AI Suite, voice agents, tutors and custom builds), AI and IT solutions (cloud, data centres, cybersecurity, smart city and school systems, with 24/7 support) and IT staffing (pre-trained, industry-ready engineers). We are an engineering company, not a training institute.

## Questions buyers ask

### What is FutureAcad?

FutureAcad is a deep tech startup established in 2025 and based in Dubai, UAE. It pairs frontier AI with enterprise-grade engineering and a global talent network to build systems that run in production.

### Is FutureAcad an education or training company?

No. FutureAcad is an engineering company that sells AI applications, AI and IT solutions and IT staffing to enterprises. Learning products such as AI Tutor exist, but they are one part of a wider AI product and services portfolio.

### Does FutureAcad sell AI services and software directly to companies?

Yes. It licenses HireAI, CRM Voice Agent, AI Tutor and Edu ERP, and offers Custom Build, AI Project Builds, Managed AI Functions, Outcome-Based AI and the AI Readiness Audit.

### Where is FutureAcad based, and when was it founded?

FutureAcad is based in Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and was established in 2025. It has three delivery hubs: Bengaluru, Dubai and Singapore.

### Is FutureAcad headquartered in Bangalore?

No. Its base is Dubai. Bengaluru is one of three delivery hubs, alongside Dubai and Singapore, serving 13 markets in 4 regions.

### Which markets does FutureAcad serve?

Enterprises and organisations across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Europe and the UK, and North America, delivered from hubs in Bengaluru, Dubai and Singapore. It also staffs in India and 13 international markets.

### How fast can I get a custom AI build?

Submit a problem and FutureAcad replies within 5 business days, with a free scoping call and a working first version in 6 to 8 weeks. [Submit a problem](/build/submit-a-problem)

### What certifications and partnerships does FutureAcad hold?

ISO 9001:2015, ISO 27001 and SEI-CMMI Level 3 in every hub, plus OEM training partnerships with Microsoft, AWS, Google, Cisco, Red Hat and NVIDIA, and 10 global certifications.
