## What a mid-sized company should check in any AI readiness audit

Many assessments stop at a score or a slide deck. Use these five checks to tell a plan you can fund from a report that sits on a shelf.

### Is the roadmap costed?

*Costed roadmap*

FutureAcad delivers a phased 12-month roadmap with costs and a business case, so finance can approve it. It is not a list of ideas.

### Are use cases ranked by value?

*NPV ranking*

A longlist of 15–25 candidates is scored on NPV. The top three get rough-order-of-magnitude build estimates and a build, buy or partner call.

### Who does the work?

*Senior team of 3*

A Transformation Lead, an AI Architect and a People & Skills Lead run it. One named person owns each part, and the lead defends the roadmap to your board.

### What does it ask of your team?

*Light on your time*

A sponsor, plus 8–15 stakeholder interviews in week one. There is no long internal project to staff.

### What happens after?

*Plan to build*

The fee is credited if you engage within 90 days. FutureAcad can also build what the audit recommends, through [AI Project Builds](/build/ai-project-builds) or a [Custom Build](/build/custom-ai-builds).
