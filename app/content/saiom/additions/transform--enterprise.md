## How does an Indian manufacturer or retailer start AI?

Start with a diagnosis, not a tool. Run the **4-week AI Readiness Audit**. It gives you an AI maturity score, a capability gap report, your top three use cases ranked by NPV and a costed 12-month roadmap.

Then pick one pre-built use case from your sector playbook and ship it as a **fixed-scope build of 4, 8 or 24 weeks**. Each build has a named delivery lead and 30 days of hypercare. Upskilling and an embedded FutureAcad pod follow, so your own teams run it.

Price is scoped on a call. We don't sell slideware: the aim is one system live in your workflow, not a deck.

## From first call to a working system

1. **Scoping conversation**: Tell us the operational problem: stock-outs, line downtime, slow customer response. If it doesn't fit a pre-built use case, the scoping call can still lead to a custom build.
2. **4-week AI Readiness Audit**: You get a maturity score, a gap report, three use cases ranked by NPV and a costed 12-month roadmap. This is the business case your finance team needs before approving spend.
3. **Pick one use case**: Choose the highest-NPV item from the audit, ideally one a pre-built sector playbook already covers. A narrow first scope gets you to production faster.
4. **Fixed-scope build**: An AI Project Build runs 4, 8 or 24 weeks with a named delivery lead. Scope and timeline are fixed up front, and 30 days of hypercare follow go-live.
5. **Upskill and embed**: Your staff are trained through AI Forward courses and a FutureAcad pod works inside your team. AI Workforce Readiness runs 12 to 24 weeks and measures productivity uplift.

## Which starting route fits your situation

All three can lead into the same enterprise programme. They differ in how much you commit and how you pay.

### Audit first

*Best when priorities are unclear*

AI Readiness Audit, 4 weeks. Output: maturity score, capability gap report, top three use cases by NPV, costed 12-month roadmap. Scoped on a call.

### Fixed-scope build

*Best when you know the use case*

AI Project Build of 4, 8 or 24 weeks. Fixed scope and timeline, named delivery lead, 30 days of hypercare after go-live.

### Custom Build

*Best when no playbook fits*

Submit a problem and get a reply within 5 business days, plus a free scoping call. A working first version follows in 6 to 8 weeks. A standard build is free to build, with monthly hosting and usage after go-live. An exclusive build carries a basic development fee.

[See Custom Build pricing](/build/custom-ai-builds/pricing)

### Pay on results

*Best when you want shared risk*

Outcome-Based AI runs on gain-share, shared-savings or per-outcome terms. You pay when a measured business result appears. Managed AI Functions bill per seat, transaction or month against SLAs.

[See Outcome-Based AI](/transform/outcome-based-ai)

## Mid-market plants, large enterprises and retail operators

FutureAcad's managed and outcome-based models are built for Indian mid-market firms (50 to 500 employees), Indian large enterprises (1,000+) and global enterprises. Manufacturers usually begin with a shop-floor or planning problem. Retailers and e-commerce sellers usually begin with a customer-facing or inventory problem. The audit ranks these by NPV, so the choice rests on numbers rather than enthusiasm.

If your data and security teams need assurance before anything goes live, delivery hubs in Bengaluru, Dubai and Singapore work to ISO 9001:2015, ISO 27001 and SEI-CMMI Level 3. If you lack in-house AI engineers, a dedicated pod of 3 to 6 India-based engineers can be added on a monthly bill. See [GCC Services](/gcc-services).

## Starting AI in a manufacturing or retail business: your questions

### How can a manufacturing or retail company in India start an AI transformation?

Begin with the 4-week AI Readiness Audit, tuned to your sector. It ends with a maturity score, a gap report, the top three use cases ranked by NPV and a costed 12-month roadmap. You then deploy one pre-built use case as a fixed-scope build, upskill your teams and embed a FutureAcad pod.

### How long until the first AI system is live?

A fixed-scope AI Project Build takes 4, 8 or 24 weeks, depending on scope, and is followed by 30 days of hypercare. If you need something custom, the first working version of a Custom Build arrives in 6 to 8 weeks.

### What does AI transformation cost for a mid-sized Indian company?

Enterprise programmes are scoped on a call by sector, team size and the use cases you choose, and we don't publish a fixed fee. For custom work, a standard build is free to build, with monthly hosting and usage after go-live. An exclusive build carries a basic development fee.

### Can we pay only if the AI delivers results?

Yes, for suitable use cases. Outcome-Based AI is delivered on gain-share, shared-savings or per-outcome terms, so you pay when a measured business result appears. Managed AI Functions are billed per seat, transaction or month against SLAs.

### Do we need our own data science team to begin?

No. The audit is the first step, and FutureAcad then builds and embeds a pod inside your workflows while upskilling your existing staff through AI Forward courses. You can add a dedicated pod of 3 to 6 engineers if you want more capacity.
