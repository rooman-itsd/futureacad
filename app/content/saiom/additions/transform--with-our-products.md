## Measure AI impact against a baseline you take before go-live

To measure the real impact of an AI tool, record today's numbers before launch, then compare the same numbers every quarter after rollout. FutureAcad works this way on every product rollout: it takes the baseline during the assess-and-match step and reviews what got faster, cheaper or better each quarter.

For enterprises in India, judge any AI platform on three things: does it connect to the systems you already run, does the vendor train the people who use it, and will the vendor commit to measuring results? FutureAcad's four products (HireAI, CRM Voice Agent, AI Tutor and Edu ERP) and its Custom Build are rolled out with all three.

## One baseline metric per product, reviewed every quarter

Pick the number the tool is meant to move, write it down before go-live, and check it on a fixed schedule. These are the starting points we use for each product.

### HireAI

*Hiring teams*

Baseline: how long screening takes and how many recruiter hours it uses. Track: time to shortlist and how much recruiter time goes to candidates who don't progress.

[See HireAI](/build/hireai)

### CRM Voice Agent

*Sales and support*

Baseline: share of leads called and how fast follow-ups happen. Track: leads called and qualified, calls logged in the CRM, and hand-offs to your team.

[See CRM Voice Agent](/build/product/crm-voice-agent)

### AI Tutor

*Education and training*

Baseline: how training is delivered and completed today. Track: learner progress, which the product records for each person, across its 50+ languages.

[See AI Tutor](/build/product/ai-tutor)

### Edu ERP

*Colleges and campuses*

Baseline: how long admissions, fee collection and academic admin take on spreadsheets. Track: the same processes once they run on one platform.

[See Edu ERP](/build/product/edu-erp)

### Custom Build

*Anything else*

Baseline: the process you named when you submitted the problem. Track: the same measure on the working first version, rolled out the same way.

[See Custom Build](/build/custom-ai-builds)

## What an enterprise AI platform in India should include

### Integration

- **Connects to your systems**: Ask which of your CRM, HRMS, ERP or LMS it integrates with, and who does the work. FutureAcad configures each product to your workflow and connects it.
- **Process redesign**: The workflow is redesigned around the tool, so the gain shows up in your numbers and not just in a demo.

### Training

- **Hands-on user training**: The people who use the tool learn it hands-on. Teams that need new skills take AI Forward courses.
- **Wider workforce programme**: For a company-wide rollout, [AI Workforce Readiness](/transform/ai-workforce-readiness) runs 12 to 24 weeks: it assesses AI maturity, upskills staff, embeds AI pods and measures productivity uplift.

### Measurement

- **Baseline and quarterly review**: Numbers are taken before go-live and reviewed every quarter, so you can see what changed and what to roll out next.

### Trust

- **Security and compliance**: Ask for certifications. FutureAcad states ISO 9001:2015, ISO 27001 and SEI-CMMI Level 3 in every delivery hub (Bengaluru, Dubai, Singapore).

## Measuring AI impact and choosing an enterprise AI platform

### How can I measure the real impact of an AI tool after deploying it across my teams?

Take a baseline before go-live: the current numbers for the process the tool is meant to improve, such as screening time, share of leads called or admissions turnaround. After rollout, compare the same numbers every quarter. FutureAcad does this for each product rollout and reviews what got faster, cheaper or better, and what to roll out next.

### What should I record in the baseline?

Record the numbers for the process itself, not the tool: how long it takes, how much staff time it uses, and how often it stalls. Write them down while the old process is still running, because you can't rebuild them afterwards. FutureAcad takes this baseline during its assess-and-match step.

### What are the best generative AI platforms for enterprises in India that include integration and user training?

Judge platforms on whether the vendor integrates with your CRM, HRMS, ERP or LMS, trains the people who use the tool, and measures results afterwards. FutureAcad covers all three. It deploys HireAI, CRM Voice Agent, AI Tutor, Edu ERP or a custom build, connects it to your systems, trains your staff hands-on and reviews results against a baseline every quarter.
