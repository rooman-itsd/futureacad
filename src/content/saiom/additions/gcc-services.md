## Choosing a GCC-as-a-service provider in Bengaluru

FutureAcad is a GCC-as-a-Service option for startups, SMEs and mid-market companies. You start with one dedicated pod of 3–6 engineers on a monthly bill, delivered from Bengaluru (with Dubai and Singapore as the other hubs). You need no Indian entity on day one, and a Build–Operate–Transfer exit into ownership over 24–36 months is written into the contract.

If you would otherwise open your own centre, the pod route lets you test India delivery first. You can then scale pod by pod, or convert to a captive centre when the model has proved itself.

## In-house GCC, staffing partner or managed pods?

The three routes differ in what you commit to up front and in what you own at the end.

### Build your own GCC

*Full control, biggest bet*

You set up a local entity, lease space and hire a leadership team before the first line of code. A traditional India GCC takes 12–18 months to reach that point. It suits companies certain of their long-term headcount.

### Staffing partner

*Fast seats, thin capability*

Permanent placement, contract hiring or employer-of-record staffing fills roles quickly. The team may not hold your product knowledge, and it can leave when the contract ends. FutureAcad also offers this through [Staffing Services](/staffing-gcc).

[See staffing services](/staffing-gcc)

### Managed pod with BOT

*Start small, own later*

A named pod works in your repos and standups while FutureAcad handles hiring, payroll, infrastructure, attrition and the bench. When you are ready, the team, entity, office and IP transfer to you on terms fixed at signing.

## How a startup builds an India engineering team without its own entity

Under the pod model, FutureAcad employs the engineers and runs payroll, infrastructure and compliance. The pod is badged to you, so it works inside your tools and time zone. You sign a Statement of Work, NDA and IP assignment, and the first production ship is targeted for week 8.

An entity only becomes a question if you choose Build–Operate–Transfer. FutureAcad then sets up the entity, office and leadership under your brand and transfers them at the end of the term. Until then you can scale, shrink or exit using the contract's exit clause.

## What to ask any GCC-as-a-service provider before you sign

### Structure

- **How small can we start?**: One Pod-S: a tech lead and two engineers, on a 6-month trial with agreed success measures.
- **Do we need an Indian entity?**: Not at the start. An entity is created only if you convert to Build–Operate–Transfer.

### Exit

- **Are the transfer terms fixed up front?**: Yes. Terms for transferring the team, entity, office and IP are set at signing, not renegotiated later.

### Assurance

- **What compliance evidence can you show?**: FutureAcad states ISO 9001:2015, ISO 27001 and SEI-CMMI Level 3 in every hub, and offers an evidence pack for European buyers.

### Talent

- **Where do the engineers come from?**: From a 1.3M+ engineer alumni pool across three hubs (Bengaluru, Dubai, Singapore), trained on your stack before day one.

### Commercials

- **What does it cost?**: A monthly bill per pod. Pricing is shared after a 30-minute pod sizing call, not published as a list.
