## A first-round workflow that protects quality

1. **Fix the bar before you invite**: Agree the questions and rubric for the role with the hiring manager, or start from a template, so every candidate is judged the same way.
2. **Let candidates interview asynchronously**: Send private links in bulk. Candidates answer in the browser, with no download and no scheduling.
3. **Review by exception**: Sort by score, then check proctoring flags and communication scores to see who needs a closer look.
4. **Watch and read only the top tier**: Use recordings and transcripts to confirm the scores. Humans make the decision, with evidence in front of them.
5. **Recalibrate**: If shortlisted candidates keep failing the next round, tighten the rubric or questions and run the next batch against the new bar.

## What to check in any AI interview platform for screening at scale

### Scoring

- **Rubric-based, not a single opaque score**: You should see how each answer was scored and be able to review the feedback behind it.

- **Recordings and transcripts you can search**: Reviewers outside the recruiting team can verify a score without re-interviewing.
