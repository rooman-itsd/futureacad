## What are AI apps, and which ones do organisations actually use?

An AI app is a piece of software where AI does the core work: talking, teaching, scoring, searching or deciding. It sits inside how your organisation already runs, rather than beside it.

Most real-world use falls into a handful of types. Voice agents handle calls. Tutors teach. Campus platforms run schools and colleges. Hiring tools screen candidates. Custom internal tools cover everything else.

Below, each type is set out the same way: the problem, how it works, and where to go next.

## Five types of AI app and what each one does

### AI tutors

*Learners*

**The problem:** learners need one-to-one help, and there are never enough teachers to give it.

**How it works:** AI Tutor is a voice-first learning companion in 50+ languages. It builds personal learning paths, delivers microlearning and uses Socratic dialogue to get learners thinking rather than just giving answers.

[See AI Tutor](/build/product/ai-tutor)

### Voice agents

*Sales and service*

**The problem:** calls go unanswered, leads go cold and follow-up depends on who is free.

**How it works:** CRM Voice Agent is an autonomous AI voice agent that makes and receives calls 24/7. It scores leads 0–100, logs every call in your CRM and transfers to AI or human agents when needed.

[See CRM Voice Agent](/build/product/crm-voice-agent)

### Campus and ERP platforms

*Schools and colleges*

**The problem:** admissions, academics, assessment and fees live in separate tools, so staff spend their time stitching them together.

**How it works:** Edu ERP is a cloud SaaS campus management platform covering admissions, academics, assessment, finance, outreach and centre networks, with NAAC and AICTE report exports.

[See Edu ERP](/build/product/edu-erp)

### Hiring and interview tools

*Hiring teams*

**The problem:** screening large candidate pools by hand is slow and inconsistent.

**How it works:** HireAI is an AI interview and recruitment platform. It scores candidates against a rubric, with proctoring, recording, transcription and shortlisting built in.

[See HireAI](/build/hireai)

*Your own workflow*

**The problem:** the process that slows you down is specific to you, and no off-the-shelf app fits it.

**How it works:** you describe the problem and FutureAcad builds the application around it. Choose [Custom Build](/build/custom-ai-builds) or a fixed-scope [AI Project Build](/build/ai-project-builds) of 4, 8 or 24 weeks.

[See Custom Build](/build/custom-ai-builds)

## How to choose the right AI app

Start with the problem, not the technology. Ask where your team loses the most time or where customers wait the longest.

- **Calls and leads slipping?** Look at a voice agent.
- **Learners need more support?** Look at an AI tutor.
- **Admin spread across too many tools?** Look at a campus platform.
- **Hiring is a bottleneck?** Look at an AI interview platform.
- **None of these fit?** Build something custom.
