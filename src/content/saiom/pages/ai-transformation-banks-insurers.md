*Back office*

*Front line*

*Risk*

## How an engagement runs

1. **Audit (4 weeks)**: The [AI Readiness Audit](/transform/ai-readiness-audit) gives you an AI maturity score, a capability gap report, your top three use cases ranked by NPV, and a costed 12-month roadmap.
2. **Pick the first use case**: We match your requirement to a FutureAcad AI product or a [custom build](/build/custom-ai-builds), and agree the baseline we will measure against.
3. **Build (4, 8 or 24 weeks)**: A fixed-scope, fixed-timeline build with a named delivery lead. Integration with your core systems is part of the work.
4. **Go live with hypercare**: 30 days of hypercare after launch, and training so your teams can use the system from day one.
5. **Run and scale**: Keep it in-house with an embedded pod, hand it to us as a managed AI function, or pay on [outcome-based terms](/transform/outcome-based-ai) where you only pay when a measured result appears.

## Outcomes we measure

Every programme is measured against a baseline taken before launch. These are the measures we agree with BFSI teams up front.

### Turnaround time

How long a document, claim or request takes from arrival to decision, before and after.

### Cost per transaction

The cost of handling each case, which also sets the terms for managed or outcome-based pricing.

### Service coverage

How many calls and queries are handled, and how many need a human.

### Review quality

Accuracy of extraction, and how well flagged compliance and fraud cases hold up under analyst review.
